Brand Identity and Company Profile Design for Makmel Winery

I designed a comprehensive brand identity and company profile for Makmel Winery in Lebanon, helping them build brand recognition and support sales growth.

Confidential Client Makmel
Date December 1, 2022
Duration 3 weeks

Project Details

Overview

I designed a cohesive brand identity and a professional company profile for Makmel, an established winery located in Lebanon. Using professional design tools, I developed the winery's visual positioning to help them connect with potential partners and customers. This branding project was structured to provide the client with polished, consistent marketing materials that clearly convey their heritage and wine-making expertise.

The Problem

Makmel is a long-standing winery with its own vineyards, yet they struggled to build strong market recognition. The primary challenge was a lack of high-quality marketing materials. Without a structured brand identity or a professional company profile, the winery could not effectively present its heritage, product range, or unique value proposition to distributors and consumers in a competitive marketplace.

My Approach

To address the winery's needs, I structured my process into distinct stages over a three-week period. First, I analyzed the winery's brand history and market position to determine the visual direction. Next, I established a curated color scheme using Adobe Color Plate to represent the rich heritage of the vineyard. From there, I drafted the core brand identity elements and designed a comprehensive company profile layout. By focusing on both visual aesthetics and commercial utility, I ensured the materials would serve as functional sales tools.

The Solution

The completed design solution delivered a unified brand presence. I developed:

  • A cohesive brand identity system that establishes a consistent look and feel across all touchpoints.
  • A professional company profile that details the winery's history, vineyard assets, and product offerings.
  • Digital and print-ready graphic assets optimized for distribution to potential clients and partners.

Technical Stack

I utilized standard industry tools to design, refine, and organize the visual assets for this branding project:

  • Adobe Photoshop: I used this software to edit, refine, and prepare high-resolution imagery and visual assets for the company profile.
  • Adobe Illustrator: I utilized this tool to construct the vector elements and layouts for both the brand identity and the company profile document.
  • Adobe Color Plate: I used this platform to curate and define the specific color palette, ensuring color harmony and brand consistency across all marketing assets.

Results & Impact

The delivery of these marketing materials provided Makmel with a structured, professional way to present their brand. With a clear brand identity and a detailed company profile, the winery was equipped to approach new markets and distributors. Following the implementation of these graphic design assets, the client reported higher sales, demonstrating the practical value of establishing a professional and recognizable market presence.

Key Takeaways

  • Cohesive visual identity: I established a consistent design system that reflects the winery's long-standing heritage.
  • Professional market presence: The newly designed company profile allows the client to present their business clearly to potential partners.
  • Structured brand assets: Using professional design tools, I delivered organized, high-quality collateral ready for both print and digital distribution.
  • Commercially focused design: The marketing materials directly supported the client's business growth, resulting in reported sales increases.
