Branding and Visual Identity Design for Brilliant Solutions

I developed a comprehensive visual identity and branding system for Brilliant Solutions. This included logo design, print collateral, and social media assets to elevate their professional presence.

Brilliant Solutions Brilliant Solutions
Date June 15, 2022
Duration 4 months

Project Details

Overview

I worked with Brilliant Solutions, a PR and services firm operating in Dubai and Abu Dhabi, to refine their visual identity. They needed a cohesive design system that represented their growth and professional stature in a competitive market.

My role was to align their brand's visual appearance with their established reputation. This project was a foundational step in ensuring their identity felt modern and authoritative across both physical and digital touchpoints.

The Problem

Although the company had been active in the UAE market for several years, they lacked a unified visual language. Their materials were inconsistent, and they had no digital infrastructure to support professional communication.

This lack of formal branding made it difficult to maintain a consistent image for potential clients. They needed a set of design standards that could communicate professionalism effectively as they expanded their operations.

My Approach

I started by analyzing their existing services and the audience in their target regions of Dubai and Abu Dhabi. My goal was to create a design that was both accessible and distinct within the local PR industry.

I conducted a series of discovery sessions to understand their core mission and values. Based on these insights, I drafted several concepts that prioritized clarity and versatility across various media formats.

The Solution

I designed a versatile logo system that serves as the centerpiece of their new identity. This was refined and finalized using Adobe Illustrator to ensure high-quality scalability for any application.

Beyond the logo, I delivered a complete brand book containing specific typeface selections and color palettes. I also designed essential print materials, including business letterheads and envelopes, to ensure professional correspondence.

Technical Stack

  • Adobe Illustrator for vector logo design
  • Adobe InDesign for brand collateral layout
  • Custom typography and font selection
  • Social media asset templates for consistent digital branding

Results & Impact

The company now has a standardized, professional look that is applied across all their communications. By establishing a clear visual identity, they have improved the consistency of their brand outreach significantly.

This project provided them with a foundation for all future growth. They no longer struggle with disjointed design, allowing them to focus entirely on delivering their public relations and management services.

Key Takeaways

  • A consistent visual identity is essential for building trust in the competitive Dubai market.
  • I focused on scalability so that the logo remains effective on everything from a business card to a billboard.
  • Simplifying the design elements helped the client maintain their brand consistency long-term.
