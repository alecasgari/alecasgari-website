Integrated Marketing Campaign and Digital Lead Automation for NK Rent Cars

I managed a comprehensive marketing campaign for NK Rent Cars and integrated automated lead generation workflows. I connected ad platforms to a central website and CRM to streamline customer acquisition.

Hamed Nikkhah NK Rent Cars
Date March 1, 2024
Duration 1 year

Project Details

Overview

I led a targeted digital marketing campaign for NK Rent Cars to boost their brand presence and rental bookings. The project centered on creating high-converting landing pages integrated directly into their lead management system.

This initiative allowed me to bridge the gap between initial customer interest and final booking. By aligning my marketing efforts with automated backend processes, I ensured a smooth experience for both the business and the customer.

The Problem

The client struggled with inefficient lead capture processes that caused missed opportunities. Before I started, their marketing activities were disconnected from their sales pipeline, leading to slow response times and potential revenue loss.

There was a clear need to automate the flow of inquiries from various platforms. I identified that manual data handling was the primary bottleneck preventing the team from scaling their operations effectively.

My Approach

I began by mapping out the entire customer journey from the first ad click to the final inquiry submission. I chose to build custom landing pages that were specifically optimized for mobile users to capture high-intent traffic.

I then designed an automated pipeline to consolidate leads from multiple ad sources. By centralizing the data, I made sure the client could access all prospect information in one place without manual data entry.

The Solution

I implemented a series of integrated landing pages on the client's existing website architecture. These pages served as the primary destination for all my ad traffic, ensuring consistent messaging and clear calls to action.

I connected these landing pages to a centralized lead generation system powered by intelligent automation. This setup enabled instant lead capture and verification, directly routing prospects into the client’s CRM.

Technical Stack

I leveraged several platforms to build this marketing and automation infrastructure. The core stack included Google Ads, TikTok Ads, and Snapchat Ads for traffic acquisition, supported by landing pages designed to maximize conversion.

On the backend, I used automated workflow tools to handle the lead routing. I also integrated an email marketing system to nurture leads efficiently, ensuring that the CRM remained the single source of truth for all interactions.

Results & Impact

By implementing this automated infrastructure, I significantly improved the conversion rates for the rental booking process. The client no longer has to handle lead entry manually, freeing up their team to focus entirely on customer service and closing deals.

This project represented a major shift in how the business handles its digital growth. The increased visibility and streamlined lead management led to a noticeable rise in rentals, demonstrating the value of connecting marketing channels to automated back-end systems.

Key Takeaways

  • Automation is essential for managing leads from multiple ad channels simultaneously.
  • Optimizing landing pages for specific demographics directly correlates to higher conversion rates.
  • Centralizing data in a CRM eliminates manual errors and improves response time to potential customers.
  • A well-integrated pipeline allows a business to scale its marketing efforts without adding administrative overhead.
