Healthcare Pitch Deck Design for UAE Market Expansion

I designed an investor pitch deck using Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop, and Canva to support a healthcare organization's expansion from India to the UAE.

Mr. Bidkhan TACA
Date
Duration 3 days

Project Details

Overview

I designed a pitch deck for TACA, a healthcare organization managed by Mr. Bidkhan. Completed over a three-day duration in January 2023, the goal of this design project was to support TACA’s plans to expand its operations from India into the United Arab Emirates and secure new investor funding.

Using design software including Canva, Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop, and curated stock photos, I developed a presentation structured to communicate TACA’s operational presence and future business strategy. The completed deck provided management with a visually polished asset tailored for investor discussions and market expansion planning.

The Problem

TACA required a tailored presentation to present its business model and growth plans to potential investors. The organization aimed to establish a new branch in the United Arab Emirates, extending its reach beyond its existing healthcare operations in India.

To effectively engage potential investors and support this international expansion, management needed a structured, visual pitch deck. The primary requirement was to organize critical business information and expansion goals into a clear presentation suitable for formal investment review.

My Approach

I structured the pitch deck design process to gather necessary requirements and turn company plans into clear visual assets within a concise three-day timeline.

First, I reviewed the core requirements for the presentation, focusing on the expansion objectives from India to the UAE. Next, I selected appropriate graphic tools and high-quality stock photography to support the visual design. Using Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop, and Canva, I established consistent design layouts, visual elements, and graphics to maintain a professional standard throughout the slides. Finally, I compiled all elements into a completed deck ready for delivery to the client.

The Solution

The delivered presentation provided TACA with a structured asset designed specifically for investor discussions regarding their United Arab Emirates branch expansion.

  • Requirement gathering: I aligned slide layouts with the expansion plan and investor presentation goals provided by company management.
  • Graphic and layout design: Using Adobe Illustrator and Photoshop, I prepared specialized graphic elements and refined visual assets to maintain clarity.
  • Slide compilation: I structured the presentation content in Canva and incorporated targeted stock photos to maintain visual consistency across all sections.
  • Delivery: I finalized and delivered the complete pitch deck to Mr. Bidkhan for use in investor recruitment and strategic meetings.

Technical Stack

I selected a combination of design applications to build graphic assets and format the presentation layout efficiently.

  • Canva: I used it to format layout structures and assemble the final slide presentation.
  • Adobe Illustrator: I used it to design vector visual assets and graphic components for the slides.
  • Photoshop: I used it to prepare and edit photo assets and adjust graphic elements.
  • Stock photos: I selected relevant imagery to support visual themes throughout the presentation.

Results & Impact

The completed pitch deck delivered a clean, structured visual presentation aligned with TACA’s international expansion goals.

The asset provided management with a presentation designed to communicate business plans to prospective investors in the United Arab Emirates. By organizing information into visual layouts, the deck supported TACA’s outreach efforts to expand beyond their established presence in India. I completed and delivered the finalized design within the three-day timeframe.

Key Takeaways

  • Targeted design: I created a pitch deck aligned with TACA's strategic goal of expanding healthcare operations from India to the UAE.
  • Multi-tool workflow: I combined Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop, and Canva to deliver polished vector graphics and presentation layouts.
  • Rapid delivery: I completed the requirements gathering, graphic preparation, and presentation structure within a three-day turnaround.
  • Investor readiness: The finalized deck provided company management with a structured asset for formal investment meetings.
