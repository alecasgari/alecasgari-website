Comprehensive BPMS Implementation for Mashoon Consulting

Designed and implemented a full-scale Business Process Management System (BPMS) to digitize workflows and automate operational processes for Mashoon Consulting.

Confidential Client Mashoon Consulting
Date May 26, 2026
Duration 1 year

Project Details

Project Overview

  • The objective was to design and deploy a complete Business Process Management System (BPMS) to transition traditional operations into a fully paperless, automated ecosystem.

The Solution

  • Digital Transformation: Successfully converted all manual processes into a digital, paperless environment.
  • Unified Platform: Integrated Sales, Marketing, and Technical departments into a single, centralized platform.
  • User Accessibility: Created distinct account roles for all users, ensuring streamlined cross-departmental collaboration.

Results

  • Efficiency Gains: The implementation removed manual bottlenecks, significantly reducing turnaround times for internal tasks.
  • Centralized Data: Established a single source of truth for all operational data, improving reporting and decision-making capabilities.
