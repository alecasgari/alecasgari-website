Full Brand Identity and Design for NK Rent Cars

I designed a complete brand identity and marketing collateral for NK Rent Cars. I created their logo, stationery, and social media templates to build a professional market presence.

Hamid Nikkhah NK Rent Cars
Date November 1, 2022
Duration 3 years

Project Details

Overview

I worked with NK Rent Cars, a car rental company based in Dubai, to build their visual foundation. My goal was to create a cohesive brand look that effectively represented their new business in a competitive market.

This project involved designing their visual identity from the ground up. I wanted to ensure their brand looked professional and trustworthy to attract their first wave of customers.

The Problem

The company was brand new and lacked any visual materials. Without a logo or defined brand style, they struggled to communicate their value and build trust with potential clients.

They had a great business model, but their marketing materials did not match the quality of their service. They needed a distinct look to stand out in the busy Dubai car rental market.

My Approach

I started by identifying the core values of the business to guide the design process. I wanted to make sure the final look was clean, modern, and aligned with the rental industry.

I broke the project into key areas, starting with the primary logo and extending into administrative and marketing assets. By finalizing the core brand identity first, I ensured all secondary materials would remain consistent.

The Solution

I delivered a full brand package, including logo design and an official brand identity set. This set included stationery, business cards, and digital templates that allowed them to maintain a uniform brand presence.

I also designed templates for their social media accounts and promotional banners for their website. This allowed the team to create content quickly without needing to hire a designer for every single post.

Technical Stack

  • Adobe Creative Suite for logo and print design
  • Figma for digital and UI assets
  • Brand identity guidelines

Results & Impact

The company launched with a polished and professional image that helped them acquire their first clients quickly. The team now has a consistent brand kit that saves them time and effort.

By having all their assets ready at launch, they could focus on their rental operations rather than worrying about design. The identity I provided gave them the confidence to market themselves effectively from day one.

Key Takeaways

  • I created a consistent visual language that builds instant brand trust.
  • Well-designed templates enable businesses to scale their social presence efficiently.
  • A strong logo is only the start; a full identity set is what really defines a brand.
