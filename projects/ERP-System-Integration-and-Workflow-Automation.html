ERP System Integration and Workflow Automation

I implemented a full ERP and resource management system for a consulting firm. I integrated the system with their internal processes to improve efficiency.

Mohannad Ebrahim Mohannad Ebrahim Consulting
Date June 20, 2026
Duration 4 months

Project Details

Overview

I designed and implemented a full ERP system for a consulting firm to manage their resources and organizational workflows.

The Problem

The company struggled with managing resources across departments. Their existing processes were slow, and communication gaps caused significant operational delays.

My Approach

I mapped out their organizational structure and internal processes. I selected an ERP solution that fit their specific requirements and planned the integration steps accordingly.

The Solution

I deployed a unified ERP system. I streamlined their data management and ensured all departments could access the information they needed within a single platform.

Technical Stack

  • ERP software suite
  • Data migration tools
  • Internal API connectors

Results & Impact

I unified the company's data infrastructure, which reduced manual work and helped the team manage resources much more effectively than before.

Key Takeaways

  • I successfully transitioned the firm to a centralized digital system.
  • Proper data mapping is the most critical step for any ERP integration.
  • I enabled better team communication by automating resource reporting tasks.
