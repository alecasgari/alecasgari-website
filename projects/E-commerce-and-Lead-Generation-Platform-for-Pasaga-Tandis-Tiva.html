E-commerce and Lead Generation Platform for Pasaga Tandis Tiva

I designed and implemented an e-commerce platform integrated with a lead generation system to help Pasaga Tandis Tiva scale their sales online.

Mehdi Amiri Pasaga Tandis Tiva
Date July 20, 2022
Duration 4 months

Project Details

Overview

I developed a complete e-commerce solution for Pasaga Tandis Tiva to modernize their digital presence. The project focused on bridging the gap between their services and a growing online customer base.

I aimed to create a platform that was not only functional but also aligned with their specific brand requirements. This was essential for establishing a professional digital footprint.

Ensuring that the platform could handle modern traffic while remaining easy to manage was a primary goal. I wanted the client to have a tool that supports long-term growth.

The Problem

The company lacked an effective online presence to manage customer inquiries and service requests. This created a disconnect between their high-quality service and potential leads.

They were relying on manual processes that were difficult to track and slow to scale. This resulted in lost opportunities and unnecessary administrative burden.

They needed a centralized system to capture interest and manage customers effectively. Without a dedicated platform, they could not communicate their value clearly to prospects.

My Approach

I started by performing a thorough analysis of their current workflow and customer interaction patterns. This helped me identify the key features required to optimize their digital presence.

I drafted the project architecture to ensure a clean user experience. I focused on intuitive navigation to ensure that potential customers could find what they needed without frustration.

I involved the client in every stage of the design process. This collaboration allowed me to tailor the interface specifically to their needs and requirements.

The Solution

I built a fully functional e-commerce portal that enabled the client to showcase their services and products clearly. The design is modern, responsive, and easy for their team to update.

I integrated a custom lead generation system that automatically captures and organizes user requests. This removed the need for manual data entry and improved tracking.

I provided a system that is robust enough for daily use while remaining simple enough for the team to maintain. This has streamlined their entire sales process significantly.

Technical Stack

  • Custom CMS for content management
  • Frontend frameworks for responsive design
  • Lead capture database for tracking inquiries
  • SMTP integrations for automated notifications

Results & Impact

By implementing this platform, I enabled the client to manage their online sales independently. They no longer need to handle inquiries manually, saving them valuable time.

This project resulted in a professional online presence that represents their brand accurately. The client has seen an increase in organized leads and improved customer satisfaction.

I helped the business transition into a more efficient digital workflow. The system has become a foundation for their future online marketing and sales efforts.

Key Takeaways

  • I successfully automated a manual lead generation process to save the team time.
  • Designing for the user is just as important as building for business functionality.
  • A well-integrated system creates a more professional experience for potential customers.
  • Clear communication throughout the project ensured we met the client's expectations perfectly.
