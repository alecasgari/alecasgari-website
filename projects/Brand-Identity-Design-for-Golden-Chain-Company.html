Brand Identity Design for Golden Chain Company

I designed a professional logo and visual identity for a food industry company. I helped them establish their brand presence through consistent design assets.

Mr. Hashemi Golden Chain Company
Date June 15, 2024
Duration 4 weeks

Project Details

Overview

I developed a professional logo and visual brand identity for Golden Chain Company. The goal was to give them a modern, recognizable look in the food industry market.

The Problem

As a newly established company, they lacked a visual identity. They had no brand assets to represent their business to potential customers.

My Approach

I started by researching their industry and needs. I created several design concepts and iterated on them based on the client's feedback.

The Solution

I designed a clean, modern logo and established a visual identity. I provided the final assets in various formats to ensure they could use them across all channels.

Technical Stack

  • Adobe Illustrator
  • Adobe Creative Suite

Results & Impact

I provided the client with a strong, cohesive look that represents their brand. They now have a professional visual identity for their marketing materials.

Key Takeaways

  • A strong brand identity is essential for a new business.
  • Iterative feedback was key to finding the right design direction.
  • Simplicity in design helps with long-term brand recognition.
