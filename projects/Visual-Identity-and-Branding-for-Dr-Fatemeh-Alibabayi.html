Visual Identity and Branding for Dr. Fatemeh Alibabayi

I designed a professional visual identity for a specialist obstetrician-gynecologist, including a custom logo, typography, and marketing assets.

Dr. Fatemeh Alibabayi Dr. Fatemeh Alibabayi Medical Clinic
Date June 21, 2022
Duration 4 weeks

Project Details

Overview

I developed a professional visual identity for Dr. Fatemeh Alibabayi, a specialist in obstetrics and gynecology. The project focused on creating a brand presence that reflects trust, professionalism, and care.

The Problem

The doctor lacked a formal digital presence and consistent visual identity. She needed a brand that would help her stand out in the medical field while feeling welcoming to patients.

My Approach

I started by researching symbols relevant to medical care and obstetrics. I focused on building a clean, modern aesthetic that communicates expertise while maintaining a patient-centric tone.

The Solution

I designed a unique logo that represents her medical practice. I also established a typography style and created essential brand assets including business cards, stationery, and other professional documents.

Technical Stack

  • Figma for design and prototyping
  • Adobe Creative Suite for final asset creation

Results & Impact

I provided Dr. Alibabayi with a cohesive brand identity. This unified look has helped her project a more professional image across all patient interactions and medical materials.

Key Takeaways

  • I translated clinical expertise into a clean visual language.
  • Consistency across business cards and stationery strengthens brand recognition.
  • Understanding the professional needs of medical specialists is vital for creating effective health-care branding.
