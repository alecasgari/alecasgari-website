Services

A Unified Stack for Leaner Operations.

We integrate and automate your core business tools into a single, intelligent system, eliminating manual work and clarifying your entire customer journey.

  • We build custom AI agents and n8n workflows to handle tasks like inbox triage, lead scoring, and data cleanup, freeing up your team for high-value work.

  • We deploy and configure your core stack—SuiteCRM, Mautic, and Nextcloud—into one seamless system for a single source of truth.

  • We manage the secure import of your existing customer data, handle field mapping, and run deduplication processes to ensure your CRM data is clean and reliable.

  • We configure SPF, DKIM, and DMARC, set up your sending domains, and implement a strategic warm-up plan to ensure your emails land in the inbox, not spam.
