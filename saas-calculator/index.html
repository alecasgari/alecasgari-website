Frequently Asked Questions

Do I truly own my data?

Absolutely. 100%. The entire software stack is installed on your private, dedicated server. We have no access to your data unless you explicitly grant it for support purposes. You own everything.

What are the hidden costs?

There are none. The $1899 is a one-time fee for the software installation, configuration, server setup, and building initial automation workflows. The only recurring cost you'll ever have is the annual domain name renewal (around $15/year), which you pay directly to the registrar.

What if I need support or new features?

We offer optional monthly support and development retainers for businesses that need ongoing help, custom features, or new automation workflows. We can discuss these if and when you need them.

Can this system scale as my business grows?

Yes. We start you on a powerful server, and all the software is built to scale. If you outgrow your initial server, upgrading is a straightforward process with minimal downtime.
