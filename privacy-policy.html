Privacy Policy

How we handle your personal information on alecasgari.com and related tools.

Last updated: 21 June 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Alec Asgari (“I”, “me”, “my”) collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information when you visit alecasgari.com, use the contact form, subscribe through the SaaS Cost Calculator at calculator.alecasgari.com, or otherwise interact with my services.

By submitting a form or using these websites, you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not submit personal data.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with the site, we may collect:

  • Contact details — name, email address, phone number (if provided), company name, and your message.
  • Form context — selected topic or service, page URL, and submission timestamp.
  • Calculator leads — email address and any details you enter when requesting results or follow-up from the SaaS Cost Calculator.
  • Technical data — IP address, browser type, device information, and pages visited, collected through analytics tools.
  • Cookies and similar technologies — used by Google Analytics to understand site usage (see Analytics below).

We do not intentionally collect sensitive categories of data (such as health, financial, or government ID information) through these forms.

How We Use Your Information

Personal data is used to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and contact requests.
  • Deliver calculator results, recommendations, or follow-up communication you requested.
  • Process form submissions through automated workflows (including n8n webhooks) for notification and lead management.
  • Improve website content, performance, and user experience.
  • Measure traffic and engagement through analytics.
  • Maintain security and prevent spam or abuse (including honeypot and basic validation on forms).

n8n Webhooks & Third Parties

When you submit a form on this website or through the Calculator email modal, data may be sent to my self-hosted automation platform (n8n) via secure webhook endpoints. From there, notifications may be delivered to email or other tools I use to manage leads.

We may also use the following third-party services:

  • Google Analytics (GA4) — usage statistics and traffic measurement.
  • Google Tag Manager — on the Calculator subdomain for tag management.
  • Hosting & infrastructure — servers and CDN providers that deliver the website.

These providers process data according to their own privacy policies. I choose services that align with reasonable security and data-protection practices.

Data Retention

Contact and lead data is kept only as long as needed to respond to your request, maintain business records, or comply with legal obligations. Analytics data is retained according to Google Analytics settings (typically 14 months unless configured otherwise).

Unsubscribe & Your Rights

If you receive marketing or follow-up emails from me, you may unsubscribe at any time by:

  • Clicking the unsubscribe link in any email (when provided), or
  • Emailing hello@alecasgari.com with the subject “Unsubscribe”.

Depending on your location, you may also have the right to request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data. I will respond to reasonable requests within a practical timeframe.

Security

I use HTTPS, access controls, and reputable hosting to protect data in transit and at rest. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but I take reasonable steps to safeguard information.

International Transfers

My services may be accessed globally. Data may be processed in the United Arab Emirates, Australia, or other countries where my service providers operate. By using the site, you understand your data may be transferred across borders.

Changes to This Policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will reflect the latest version. Continued use of the site after changes means you accept the updated policy.
